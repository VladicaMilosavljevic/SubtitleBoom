SUBTITLEBOOM — TRANSCRIPTION AND TRANSLATION

INPUT
Select video or audio, a known language or automatic detection, and an installed Whisper model. TXT/SRT input is not required.

OUTPUT
Original-language speech creates *_transcribed.srt and *_transcribed.txt. English translation creates *_english.srt and *_english.txt. The timestamps option additionally creates *_timed.txt.

FORMATION
Whisper tokens are grouped into complete words; a word is never split between cues. Punctuation is preserved. Cues target at most two lines and 44 characters per line, preferring sentence, clause and speech-pause boundaries. Status considers confidence and estimated reading speed. Output opens automatically in the editor.

TRANSLATION LIMIT
Whisper's Translate task translates supported speech to English. Translation to Serbian, Croatian or another target language requires a separate translation model or service.

SERBIAN SCRIPT
Serbian Latin and Serbian Cyrillic are selected in the main language list. Cyrillic conversion occurs after whole words are assembled, including lj/љ, nj/њ and dž/џ. It applies only to Serbian Cyrillic. Alignment compares Serbian scripts internally without changing the user's source text. Croatian remains Latin-only. Whisper's word choices and foreign letters are not automatically corrected.

MODEL GUIDANCE
Tiny: alignment and fast processing. Base: balanced speed and accuracy. Small and larger: demanding transcription and translation with longer processing time.
