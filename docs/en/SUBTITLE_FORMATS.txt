SUBTITLEBOOM — SUPPORTED SUBTITLE FORMATS

IMPORT
TXT (untimed plain text), SRT, WebVTT, YouTube SBV, ASS/SSA and TTML/DFXP. Advanced ASS/SSA styles may be simplified during conversion.

EXPORT
SRT, WebVTT, YouTube SBV, ASS with a default style, and plain-text TXT.

File > Export YouTube Package creates SRT, VTT and SBV together. Batch always creates SRT and can optionally create VTT and SBV in the same project folder.

SRT, SBV and TXT do not preserve advanced ASS/SSA styling. Keep the original styled file as a backup.
