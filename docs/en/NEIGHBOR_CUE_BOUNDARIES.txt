SUBTITLEBOOM — NEIGHBOR CUES ON THE WAVEFORM

The waveform shows the previous cue in gray, the selected cue in blue, the next cue in gray, detected speech in green and the playback position as a red line.

Prevent overlap with neighboring subtitles is enabled by default. When enabled, selected boundaries cannot cross adjacent cues. When disabled, overlap is possible, but selected edges turn red and confirmation displays a warning.

Current gaps to previous and next cues appear at the bottom. Clicking or dragging a gray cue selects it for direct editing. Changes are saved immediately and enter Undo/Redo history.
